04 · projects // fleet

Projects

featured · ongoing research 2025 – Present

Multi-Model ML Framework for Aircraft Engine Health Monitoring

A three-model Prognostics & Health Management (PHM) pipeline — an LSTM Autoencoder for unsupervised anomaly detection, an LSTM RUL (Remaining Useful Life) predictor, and an XGBoost fault classifier with SHAP explainability — trained on NASA's C-MAPSS and N-CMAPSS turbofan engine degradation datasets.

  • RMSE of 15.02 cycles on C-MAPSS FD001, improving on the published LSTM baseline of Zheng et al. (2017) at 16.14.
  • 93.8% critical fault detection rate with a 0.0% false alert rate.
  • Five-tier risk fusion engine (NOMINAL → WATCH → ADVISORY → ACTION → AOG) aligned with DGCA CAR-M requirements for CAMO decision support.
  • Extended to NASA N-CMAPSS (2021) with real flight profiles and India-specific calibration — ISA +20°C correction and 1.30× utilisation scaling for Indian LCC operations.
  • Deployed as a React fleet-monitoring dashboard backed by a FastAPI inference server. Research paper drafted (9 sections, 15 IEEE references).
